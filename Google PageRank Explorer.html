Damping d = 0.85 d is the chance the surfer follows a link; 1 − d is a random jump to any page. Try 0.99: convergence slows down, because the second eigenvalue creeps toward 1.

Google matrix G (column = from, each column sums to 1)
How it works. Each column of the link matrix holds the probabilities of a surfer moving from that page. A page with no links jumps anywhere with equal chance. Mixing in the random jump gives G = d·M + (1−d)/n, whose dominant eigenvalue is exactly 1. Every step multiplies the score vector by G (the power method). The scores stop changing when G·r = r, which is the steady state and the eigenvector for λ = 1.
